kanal uploader

kanal uploader is a private tool used by the owner of a single YouTube channel to upload and manage that channel's own educational videos (calm, sleep-friendly science and history lessons) via the YouTube Data API. Every video is reviewed and approved by the channel owner before publishing.

It is not a public service and is not offered to other users.

Source code: github.com/abinitiomao-lang/kanal
